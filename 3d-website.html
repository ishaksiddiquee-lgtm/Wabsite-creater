How It Works

From creative concept to a live 3D website that stops people mid-scroll — here's the journey.

1

Creative Direction

We define your brand's 3D vision — the mood, colour palette, motion style, and which 3D elements will create the most impact for your audience.

2

3D Asset Design

3D models, scenes, and animations are designed and optimised — either custom-built or sourced from professional 3D libraries and refined for your brand.

3

Build, Animate & Integrate

The full site is hand-coded with Three.js, GSAP, and ScrollTrigger — including all selected features like AI agents, login, and messaging systems.

4

Performance Test & Launch

Thorough testing on mobile, tablet, and desktop. Assets are compressed and lazy-loaded before we go live — fast and flawless.
