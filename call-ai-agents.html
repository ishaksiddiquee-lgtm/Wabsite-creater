Natural Conversation Flow

The agent handles interruptions, pauses, topic switches, and objections — exactly like a trained human receptionist on a live call would.

Appointment Booking

Integrated with Google Calendar or Calendly — the agent checks real-time availability and confirms bookings during the call without any human involvement.

Inbound & Outbound Calls

Seamless Human Handoff

When a call needs a human touch, the agent smoothly transfers the caller and instantly notifies you via WhatsApp or email with full conversation context.

Multi-Language Support

Serve customers in their own language — the agent can switch languages naturally mid-call, expanding your reach without hiring multilingual staff.
